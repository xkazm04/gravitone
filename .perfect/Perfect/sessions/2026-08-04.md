## What the round actually found

**Builders falsified brief claims FIVE times and were right every time** (the
round-7 lesson holding at full strength):
1. E1: submit() double-counted the job's own render — every promise the
   service ever stamped was inflated by one render. Invisible until promises
   were measured.
2. E2: cache.py stats() was not the RuntimeError risk claimed (len+ints are
   GIL-atomic); the real bug was inconsistent sampling across resize.
3. E2: forwarding XFF naively would have OPENED a spoof hole — trusted-hops
   counted from the right was required to make the fix safe.
4. P1: <audio src> streaming unviable (8000-char POST body vs Node's 16KB
   request-line cap; MSE supports no wav) → PCM16 scheduling on the shared
   AudioContext instead, reusing _live/conversation's shape.
5. P1: streaming cannot be the solo DEFAULT — /stream has no metatag grammar,
   so a tagged take would silently lose every emotion. canStream gates
   untagged+solo+wav only.

E3 self-falsified its own docstring before shipping ("byte-identical across
worker counts" is false by design — workers participate in segmentation seams
and therefore digests).
